## 9. Message chot gui DEV

Rebuild the homepage as a production-ready product front, not as a technical shell. Keep the existing PWA/mobile-first design strengths, but move technical loading states out of the main narrative. The homepage must explain CIOS as a Customer Intelligence Operating System with intelligence, compliance, constitutional control, decision support, and execution handoff. It must be honest about the current live foundation while clearly showing the operating surfaces coming online. Do not lead with APIs, version endpoints, or loading placeholders. Lead with product truth, operational value, and trust.
